This file lists the maintainers of k8s-bridge, as referenced by the
[Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Maintainers are listed by GitHub handle (not personal email) so the record is
public-safe. The lead maintainer is listed below; more will be added as the project grows.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who have shown sustained, high-quality involvement may be nominated
by an existing maintainer and confirmed by maintainer consensus. Governance will
formalize as the project matures.

## Security contact

Security reports go through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not to
individuals listed here.
