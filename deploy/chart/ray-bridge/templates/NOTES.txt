*** webhook.enabled is FALSE ***
Inner RayJobs that omit spec.entrypointResources will schedule immediately
on the shared RayCluster — no Kueue admission, no quota check, no pool
mapping. See this chart's README.md ("Without the webhook, Kueue admission
is opt-in, not enforced") before relying on ray-bridge for admission control
in this namespace.
{{- end }}

1. Watch the rollout (Recreate strategy — expect a brief gap while the old
   pod terminates before the new one starts):

     kubectl rollout status deployment/{{ include "ray-bridge.fullname" . }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

2. Confirm the controller is Ready:

     kubectl get pods -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}
     kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} deploy/{{ include "ray-bridge.fullname" . }} 8081:8081
     curl -s localhost:8081/readyz

3. Metrics are served on port 8080 of the same pod (no dedicated Service is
   rendered for it by this chart, unlike k8s-bridge's — port-forward
   directly or add your own scrape config):

     kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} deploy/{{ include "ray-bridge.fullname" . }} 8080:8080
     curl -s localhost:8080/metrics

{{- if .Values.webhook.enabled }}

4. The admission webhook is enabled. Confirm its certificate is ready and
   the MutatingWebhookConfiguration has picked up the CA bundle:

     kubectl get certificate {{ include "ray-bridge.webhookCertificateName" . }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
     kubectl get mutatingwebhookconfiguration {{ include "ray-bridge.fullname" . }}-auto-suspend -o jsonpath='{.webhooks[0].clientConfig.caBundle}'

   And that your workload namespace(s) carry the label the webhook's
   namespaceSelector requires:

     kubectl label namespace {{ .Values.config.namespace }} ray-bridge.x-k8s.io/managed=true --overwrite
{{- end }}

5. Full submission mechanics (the pin-gate model, RayJob annotations, and
   the without-webhook manual procedure) live in
   deploy/ray-bridge/README.md in the parent repo.
